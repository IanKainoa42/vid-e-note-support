# Vid-e-Note Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-05-20_

Vid-e-Note ("the app") is a single-user iPad and iPhone application for drawing and annotating on videos. It is developed by Ian Richardson.

## Summary

**Vid-e-Note does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.** All processing happens on your device.

## Data the app handles

- **Videos** you open from your Photos library or Files. They are read into memory on your device only. The app does not upload them anywhere.
- **Annotations** (pen strokes, shapes, notes) you draw over a video. These are stored on your device.
- **Exported MP4 files** you choose to save to Photos. The export runs entirely on your device.

The app makes **no network requests**. It contains **no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no third-party trackers, and no crash reporting beyond what Apple provides at the OS level** if you opt in to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings.

## Permissions

- **Photo Library (Add)** — requested only when you tap the share / export action. Used to save the annotated MP4 you produced. The app does not read your existing photo library.

## Children

Vid-e-Note is rated 4+ and does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

## Account & sign-in

There is no account. There is no sign-in.
